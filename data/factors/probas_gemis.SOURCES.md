## What ProBas actually is, and why most of it had to be excluded

ProBas is a soda4LCA node, the same REST family named in the brief
(Oekobaudat, JRC LCDN). Two very different kinds of content live behind
the same `/resource/processes` endpoint:

1. **Republished ecoinvent.** A large fraction of ProBas2's processes
   (named `Markt für <X>; Technologiemix; Produktionsmix, ab Werk`) carry
   `dataSources: [{"name": "ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off"}]` in their own search
   listing. `resource/datastocks` even names one of its four data stocks
   `Probas2_Aufstockung2`, self-described as "test data stock to try
   import of ~200 processes provided by ecoinvent in ilcd format" —
   confirming the mechanism. **Every such process is excluded outright**,
   per the project's ecoinvent rule, by `is_commercial_sourced()` — checked
   against both the search-listing `dataSources` field and the full
   process detail's own `referenceToDataSource` list, independently, so a
   process cannot slip through by omitting the tag in one place and not
   the other.
2. **Original GEMIS content.** Roughly 20 000 remaining processes are
   carried over from **GEMIS** ("Globales Emissions-Modell Integrierter
   Systeme"), a model built and maintained by the Öko-Institut since 1987
   and commissioned by UBA (`administrativeInformation.commissionerAndGoal`
   names "Umweltbundesamt" / project "GEMIS-Stammdaten" on every process
   read here). These processes cite their own literature (Ullmann's
   Encyclopedia of Industrial Chemistry, BUWAL 1991, ECN 2005, Römpp,
   ESU/PSI/BEW 1996, ...), not ecoinvent, not GaBi/Sphera. This is the
   pool the seven kept rows come from.

Two processes (the *plain* `Ammoniak` and `Wasserstoff` — not the
`Chem-Anorg\...-DE-<year>` vintages) sit awkwardly between these two
categories: their own `dataSources` field is empty, but their full-detail
`referenceToDataSource` list names "Ecoinvent 2.0" as one of roughly a
dozen citations (alongside IPCC 2007, WMO 1991/1995/1999, Hauschild 98,
FAOStat 2008, DESTATIS, ...) that read like boilerplate references for the
LCIA *characterisation* method rather than the LCI inventory itself. This
script did not have to resolve that ambiguity: both processes are also
declared per **tonne**, not per kg, so the per-kg filter excludes them
regardless — see the script's `PER_KG_RE` filter and the module docstring.
The cleaner, unambiguously non-ecoinvent, per-kg `Chem-Anorg\Ammoniak-DE-2020`
and `Chem-Anorg\H2-Stoff-DE-2015` processes are used instead.

## Rejected candidates (see `--report` for the complete machine-generated
list — summarized here)

- **Every `Markt für ...` / "ecoinvent 3.8 cut-off" process** matching any
  of the 7 search terms (toluene, water, ethanol, ammonia, hydrogen,
  acetic acid all have one) — excluded on the ecoinvent rule, see above.
- **Older/newer GEMIS vintages of a kept substance** (e.g.
  `Ammoniak-DE-2000/2005/2010/2015/2030/2050`, `Essigsäure-DE-2000`,
  `H2-Stoff-DE-2010`) — not wrong, just superseded by the preferred vintage
  per the rationale recorded in `SUBSTANCE_SPECS` in the script (most
  recent non-forecast year with the fullest literature citation).
- **`Chem-Anorg\Ammoniak-Lösung (25%)-DE-2010`** — a 25% aqueous ammonia
  solution, not anhydrous ammonia; excluded as a non-preferred variant
  (same mechanism as the vintage exclusions above; if this project ever
  wants a specific "ammonia solution" row it would need to be treated as
  a distinct product, not folded into the anhydrous-ammonia identifier).
- **`Chem-Anorg\H2-DE-2010/2015`, `Chem-Org\Methanol-DE-*`,
  `Methanol-Brenner-Formaldehyd-DE-2000`** — declared per TJ (energy
  content), not per kg, with no kg-equivalent stated in the dataset
  itself; skipped per the project's conversion rule rather than guessing
  an LHV.
- **~20 "Pkw-Otto-...-EtOH-..." biofuel/vehicle-pathway processes** — these
  are automotive fuel-ethanol life-cycle pathways declared per passenger-km
  ("P.km"), not per kg of the substance; a completely different product
  and functional unit from reagent-grade ethanol.
- **`Ammoniak` and `Wasserstoff` (plain, no vintage suffix)** — per-tonne,
  and ambiguously ecoinvent-adjacent (see above); excluded by the per-kg
  filter regardless of the ecoinvent question.

## Verification

`identifier` is the CAS number returned by PubChem (`cids/JSON` on the
English query name in `SUBSTANCE_SPECS.pubchem_query`, then
`synonyms/JSON` on the resulting CID, first CAS-shaped synonym, check
digit verified with `carbonroute.schema.cas_checksum_ok`), never typed
from memory — see `build_row()`. All seven resolve to a single PubChem
CID with a CAS-shaped synonym that passes its check digit, and all seven
CAS numbers match the ones given in the project brief's priority list
(this was not assumed — it is what PubChem's live lookup returned).

## Re-running

    PYTHONPATH=src python3 scripts/ingest_probas_gemis.py --report

Network access to `data.probas.umweltbundesamt.de` and
`pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` required. PubChem responses are cached under
`--cache-dir` (default: this round's scratch directory) so re-runs are
cheap.
